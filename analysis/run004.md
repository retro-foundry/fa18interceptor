# Human coverage run004

`captures/run004/` is a sealed replay from the preserved post-load menu state.
The player reports choosing aircraft 1 at Base 3, leaving it stationary, then
firing guns first and several missiles.

## Authority

- 259 post-setup events; final recorded frame 3,854.
- `playback.e9k` SHA-256 is recorded in `captures/run004/run.json`.
- Two ordinary replays through frame 3,854 agree exactly on CPU registers,
  cycle count, Chip RAM, Slow RAM, complete savestate, video, and audio.
- End-frame video SHA-256:
  `af537f18fc54318a052288f386c6157afefc672666e14ae0fa277021a6fcd8c2`.

## Isolated fire packets

The first Space at source frame 2,596 was isolated from a frame-2,595 restore.
It reaches `$C1AD74` as raw `$40` on replay frame 8 and returns in 83
instructions. Its `$C0833E` helper sees `$C461E7` high nibble `$10`, sets
`$C4599A` bit 2, and ORs bit 3 into `$C458C6`; it does not write `$C457BA`.
Canonical P-code is `pcode/raw/run004_first_fire_dispatch/` (83 starts, 365
operations).

The first later Space at source frame 2,932 was isolated from frame 2,931. It
also maps to raw `$40`, returns in 67 instructions, and takes the `$30`
high-nibble branch of the same helper. That branch sets `$C4599A` bit 2 and
writes `$C457BA = 1`, without setting `$C458C6` bit 3. Canonical P-code is
`pcode/raw/run004_second_fire_dispatch/` (67 starts, 268 operations).

These are two distinct selected-fire states that align with the reported
gun-then-missile experiment. The game-level meaning of `$10` versus `$30` is
not promoted beyond that evidence until an ammunition/object-update consumer
is bounded.

The `$30` request does have an immediate same-frame consumer: `$C22DB4` calls
`$C2374C`, which returns in 214 instructions and clears `$C457BA`. Its observed
route makes no direct calls and sets a bounded group of control bytes, including `$C457B7`,
`$C45797`, `$C458B0`, `$C457C5`, `$C45843/$C45844`, and `$C45858`. See
[`c2374c_selected_fire_consumer.md`](routines/c2374c_selected_fire_consumer.md).
The immediate `$C23A7E` continuation is also bounded: it takes the mode-zero
record-update route and returns to `$C22DCC` in 46 instructions. Its canonical
P-code is `pcode/raw/run004_c23a7e_second_fire_followup/`.
