## Meaning level and next evidence

The gate-to-flight transition is **scenario-backed**.  The user-reported
successful qualification makes this run the positive behavioral oracle that
run024 lacked, but the writer, storage location, and later reader of the
qualification state remain **unknown**.  The next trace must bracket the
user-visible success event (or a later menu attempt that is accepted) and log
the corresponding RAM/disk writes with writer PCs; only then may a pilot/log
field be named.

The six conditional bytes read by the selectable-missions queue builder at
`$C1AB74+$12..+$18` are all zero at frame 100 and in both deterministic
frame-14,298 final Slow-RAM snapshots.  They must therefore not be claimed as
the qualification-success state for this run.  Their broader mission-list
availability role remains a bounded dataflow observation only.

`captures/pilot_log_experiments/rookie_baseline/` is setup-only evidence, not
a completed persistence experiment: its manifest gives the same SHA-256 for
`baseline.adf` and `pilot_log_working.adf`.  No changed pilot/log bytes are
available from that directory, so it cannot support a qualification-status
claim.
