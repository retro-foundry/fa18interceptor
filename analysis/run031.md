1. Select menu item `1` to start the demo.
2. Take off and perform a barrel roll.
3. Acquire and shoot down an enemy, then barrel roll again.
4. Fly under the Golden Gate Bridge.
5. See Alcatraz, fly over San Francisco, then fly under a second bridge whose
   name is not yet supplied.

assignments. The known aircraft, enemy, landmark, and view transitions make
