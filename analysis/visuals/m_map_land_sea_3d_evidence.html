Traced M-map: land/sea evidence plane

This is the game-produced source-vector M-map shown as a rotatable plane. Green = observed land; blue = observed sea; black = uncaptured coverage, not sea. It is deliberately not presented as a recovered 3D terrain-height model: the evidence currently proves projected coastline fills, not absolute world coordinates or elevations.
