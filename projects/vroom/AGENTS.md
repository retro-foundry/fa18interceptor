# Vroom project instructions

Read [framework instructions](../../AGENTS.md), [project README](README.md) and
[recomp-first workflow](../../docs/recomp/porting_game.md).

- Keep Vroom addresses, loader mappings, controls, hooks and results here.
- Original media/observed execution remains authority. Imported snapshots are
  derived from the recorded upstream runtime; qualify their original parity.
- Keep supplied assets in ignored `local/`, generated code/output in `build/`.
- Preserve the original `amiga-recomp` checkout and the hashes of imported assets.
- Historical convergence/title-screen results are scoped evidence, not current
  full-game qualification. Investigate loaded overlays before translating them.
